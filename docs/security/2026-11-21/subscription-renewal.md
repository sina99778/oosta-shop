# Subscription estimates and reminders

- The order-detail API reads the order by current owner and keeps the existing private, no-store policy. It calculates an estimated end for a paid, fully delivered, finite-duration purchase from its saved duration and latest sold inventory timestamp. Legacy internal deliveries with all sold timestamps missing can use the recorded paid time. Gift cards, test orders and orders without trustworthy purchase or delivery evidence have no estimate.
- The existing cart endpoint accepts the original order item for the renewal action. It checks the current customer role, item ownership, delivery, and matching active product and plan before a quote and again inside the cart transaction. A stale or different plan is rejected. Cart stock, pricing, invoice and payment verification still apply through the existing checkout flow.
- When order mail is enabled and configured, the reminder sweep runs on startup and hourly. It reads due, previously unqueued candidates in batches and queues one reminder per purchased line through the existing unique outbox key. The current email must match the verified recovery email at queue time and at send time. The send worker also rechecks the current recipient, role, order, plan, and time window. The email links to the authenticated dashboard and contains no product, plan, date, order identifier or delivery credentials.
- The reminder window opens 72 hours before the estimated end and closes at the estimated end. Subscriptions of three days or shorter have no scheduled reminder. Each purchase remains independent because the storefront has no way to confirm whether a separate purchase extends an earlier subscription. The estimate reflects recorded Oosta delivery and duration. A supplier can apply a different entitlement start or expiry.
- The focused disposable-database test first reproduced the missing estimate and renewal cart request. It then covered owner and non-owner responses, current database role, estimate fallback, private cache headers, cart stock and stale-plan rejection, concurrent enqueue deduplication, ineligible recipient states, refunds, QA orders and reminders outside the window. It reproduced queueing to an unverified address before the verification requirement was added and checked that revoking verification after enqueue prevents sending. Email delivery used an in-memory sender.
