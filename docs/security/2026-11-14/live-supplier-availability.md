# Supplier stock on storefront product pages

Published ChatGPT, Gemini, CapCut and Canva with five existing supplier-mapped variants. Reused the catalog's fresh exact-match stock lookup. The variant picker displays supplier counts, and the listing uses an availability label. The stock count is the highest matching individual offer count, not a sum of supply or owned inventory. No stock or credentials were imported into the site's inventory.

The preview proxy permits only authenticated, schema-validated HTTPS quote POSTs on the existing quote path. Anonymous requests returned 401 with no-store. Public order creation remained 503. The storefront hides the supplier placeholder price and purchase controls. Supplier SKU mappings and secret data stay private.

The variant UI regression reproduced the absent stock count. The preview smoke reproduced the blocked quote route. Both passed after deployment. Full npm verify, lint, web production build, Docker web build, and both npm audits passed. Nginx validation passed. All four Persian and English product pages returned 200. No new order was created by the live checks. A private PostgreSQL backup archive was captured and its contents listing verified.

The live read found 4 ChatGPT codes, 14 six-month and 94 twelve-month Gemini Plus units, 26 CapCut offers and 48 Canva invitations at the tested moment. Prices are indicative and calculated only from usable per-currency rates. Navasan's USDT rate was usable and the six-month Gemini quote passed. The USD sample was outside its permitted age, so the other four quotes returned PRICING_UNAVAILABLE while stock remained visible. No currency was substituted. Inventory counts are snapshots and can change between requests. No supplier purchases, wallet debits, or customer payments occurred during publication.

The later Gemini catalog update disabled both Plus variants and published two exact-mapped Pro variants under the existing product URL. The live API showed 41 one-month family invitations and zero one-year activation codes. Warranty claims without a structured duration retained the catalog's no-warranty classification. The update preserved prior plan records and took a private database backup before changing the listing. Live product and API reads verified the active variants and no-store behavior.

The five fresh supplier catalogs did not contain a ChatGPT offer at exactly USD 6.50. A USD 3.85 VenteBot one-month offer has a 24-hour warranty in its title, a no-warranty statement in its description and no structured duration. It was not mapped as a warranted option, and the existing ChatGPT mapping was not changed.

The operator subsequently identified PremiKeyBot and confirmed full coverage for one month. The catalog returned USD 6.09 and 251 units. This listing's “No warranty for customer errors” is an exclusion, although the generic parser flags the wording. Release `20261114-chatgpt-warranty` applies the store-approved 30-day full coverage only to the exact supplier, SKU and reviewed fingerprint. Other offers retain their existing warranty rules. A changed fingerprint prevents matching. The live plan was published with warranty terms scoped to that one-month plan. No guarantee of upstream fulfillment speed is stated. The contract regression covered other IDs, changed fingerprints, sensitive flows and changed coverage duration. Full verify, lint, the API production build and both dependency audits passed. A private pre-change database backup was captured. The live API confirmed 30-day full coverage, supplier stock and quote-only checkout behavior.
